Task 2 explanation

To design an effective dashboard for Barcelona's traffic accident dataset (2019–2025), a top-down user-centered approach was adopted. The primary persona established is an Urban Traffic Safety Analyst working for the local municipal government or traffic authority (Guàrdia Urbana). This target user possesses high domain knowledge regarding local urban geography and safety regulations, accompanied by intermediate statistical literacy. They require intuitive visual analytics delivered via a desktop environment (1920x1080 resolution) during weekly or monthly safety assessment workflows.

In a typical operational scenario, the analyst reviews the dashboard prior to quarterly planning sessions to allocate traffic police shifts and prioritize infrastructure improvements. To directly support this goal, five core analytical questions were formulated, pairing specific analytical functions with tailored chart types: (1) See Trends via a Line Chart for monthly accident volume evolution from 2019 to 2025; (2) Compare via a Grouped Bar Chart for severe casualty concentrations across districts; (3) See Patterns via a temporal Heatmap for peak crash hours and days; (4) Relationships via a Stacked Bar Chart for severity correlated with collision types; and (5) Distribution via a Histogram/Donut Chart for driver age distributions.

By directly aligning each visualization style with the analyst's decision-making goals, the proposed dashboard transforms raw urban collision records into actionable municipal safety strategies.
